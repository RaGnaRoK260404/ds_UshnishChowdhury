## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes the relationship between trader behavior and market sentiment, based on the data science assignment from the Web3 Trading Team.

The analysis uses two key datasets:

* `historical_data.csv`: A log of over 180,000 individual trades.

* `fear_greed_index.csv`: A daily record of the market's Fear & Greed classification.

The goal is to identify how trading behavior (profitability, volume, risk) shifts in alignment with market sentiment, ultimately providing data-driven strategic signals.
